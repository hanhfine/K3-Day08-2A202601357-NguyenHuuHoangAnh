## International student support services
[Home](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/) / [Study at RMIT](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/study-at-rmit) / [International students](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/study-at-rmit/international-students) / [International student support services](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/study-at-rmit/international-students/international-student-support-services)
### RMIT Vietnam offers specialised support to all international students studying with us.
![airport airplane](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/stock/stock-places/airport-stock.jpg)
Free airport pick-up service
RMIT offers you a free airport pick-up service upon your arrival in Vietnam.
![Wefie campaign](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/students-public/wefie.jpg)
Complimentary activities
These include a welcome dinner, city tour, cultural discovery trips, cooking classes and ceramic/mosaic workshops
![two students one male and one female sitting together in a lecture hall for an educational session](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/rmit-vn-campus-moments/rmit-sg-campus-moments/2023/sg-life-on-campus-03.jpg)
Your own buddy
International students are assigned a buddy prior to their first semester. Buddies support international students for their entire stay in Vietnam.
![A group of three international students smiling at the camera](https://www.rmit.edu.vn/assets/vn/en/assets-for-production/images/international-students-in-vietnam-campus/rmit-sgs-international-students-14.jpg)
Orientation Week
Join Orientation Week for information on RMIT student services and networking activities.
